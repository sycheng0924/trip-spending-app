Trip Spending

Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to the Trip Spending app for iPhone and Android.

In short: your trips stay on your phone unless you share one. A shared trip is kept online only so that the phones in it can see it, and is deleted when it is no longer used. The app has no accounts to sign up for, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Trip Spending is made by JSVK Limited, a company in the United Kingdom, which is responsible for the data described here.

What the app stores on your phone

The trips, traveller names, expenses and payments you enter, and the most recently downloaded exchange rates. They are saved in the app's own storage. A trip that you do not share is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Shared trips

From version 1.4.1 you can share a trip, so that friends on other phones can add expenses to it. When you tap Share, or join a trip with an invite, the app:

  • signs in to Google Firebase anonymously. This creates a random ID for the app on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or any other account;
  • uploads that trip to Google Cloud Firestore, stored in London (europe-west2): its name and currency, the traveller names, the expenses (description, amount, currency, date, who paid, how it is shared, who added it), settle-up payments, and which phone IDs have joined, which traveller each is, when they joined and when each last opened the trip;
  • creates an invite code that lets others join the trip, and a recovery code that lets the person who shared it manage the trip again from another phone. The recovery code is kept on their phone and, online, only with the ID of the trip, of their phone and of their traveller.

Anyone with the invite code or link can join the trip, see it and add or change expenses, so send it only to the people you travel with. The person who shared the trip sets its name, currency and travellers, and can remove a phone or make a new code. Keep the recovery code to yourself: anyone who has it can take over the trip. Other trips on your phone are not uploaded.

To protect the service from misuse, the app uses Firebase App Check, which asks Apple (App Attest) or Google (Play Integrity) to confirm that requests come from the real app. As with any internet service, Google sees your device's IP address when the app connects. Google processes the data for us as our service provider, under the Firebase data processing terms; see also Privacy and security in Firebase.

How long shared trips are kept

The online copy of a shared trip is deleted when the person who shared it taps Stop sharing, two months after its last change, or one month after everything in it is settled, whichever comes first. A daily job deletes it within a day after that. Each phone keeps its own copy, which stays until you delete it. When you leave a shared trip, your phone's ID is removed from it; your traveller and the expenses you added stay in the trip for the others.

Internet use

Apart from shared trips, the app connects to the internet for one thing: downloading exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request asks for the current rates and contains nothing you have entered. That service can see your device's IP address; its own terms describe how it handles this.

Receipt scanning

When you scan a receipt, the app uses the camera to take one photo. The photo is read on your phone by the phone's own text recognition (Apple's Vision on iPhone, Google's ML Kit through Google Play services on Android), only the total is kept, and the photo is deleted straight away. The photo and its text are not sent to us or to anyone else. On Android, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic information to keep it working, such as the phone model, Android version, app version, how long reading took and error codes, but not the photo or its text; see Google's ML Kit data disclosure. Scanning an invite QR code also uses the camera; the picture is read on your phone and not kept.

Excel reports

When you tap Export to Excel, the app creates the file on your phone and opens the system share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

What we do with the data

Only what is needed to keep shared trips in step between the phones in them. We do not look at trips except to fix a problem, we do not sell or share the data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.

Your choices and rights

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app, to remove what it stored on your phone. Stop sharing (or leaving) a shared trip removes it, or your phone, from the online copy. Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below; please include the trip's invite code so that we can find it. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
